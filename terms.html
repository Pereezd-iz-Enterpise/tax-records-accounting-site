Effective October 1, 2026. Tax Records Accounting is a private, owner-operated command-line tool for the owner's QuickBooks Online accounting data.

Permitted use

Only the owner and people the owner authorizes may use the tool. The owner chooses the company to connect and directs each use. The tool is currently under development; production access may be unavailable.

Accounting decisions

The tool does not file tax returns or execute payments. The owner is responsible for reviewing reports, categories, and proposed record changes before relying on them. The tool is not professional accounting, tax, or legal advice.

Access and termination

Access requires authorization by a QuickBooks Online administrator. The owner may revoke it in QuickBooks and remove local files. QuickBooks use remains subject to Intuit's terms.

Support

Use the public support issue tracker. Do not post credentials or financial data in public issues.
