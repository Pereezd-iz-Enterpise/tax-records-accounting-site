This is an informational landing page. It has no Connect button and does not receive authorization codes or tokens. Production access to a live QuickBooks company is not active yet.

If Intuit sent you here after you approved access on its own Connect screen, check the local command-line tool to confirm which company and environment were connected. Sandbox access can be active while Production access is still pending.

The owner starts authorization from the local command-line tool after the production application is approved. QuickBooks displays the company and requested permissions before an administrator grants access. The application requests the Accounting scope.

Never paste a QuickBooks authorization code, access token, refresh token, or financial data into this page or a public support issue.
