Privacy Policy

HomeTerms

Effective October 1, 2026. Tax Records Accounting is a private, owner-operated tool under development.

Data and purpose

With authorization from a QuickBooks Online administrator, the tool uses the Accounting API to read company information, accounts, and reports. The owner may later direct it to create or update accounting records. It does not request the Payments scope.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in files on the owner's device with restricted file permissions until the owner removes them. Reports are written to local files only when the owner requests an export. The operator does not run a central QuickBooks data store or background synchronization service.

Third-party processing

The owner may direct OpenAI Codex to analyze selected QuickBooks data. That processing is governed by the owner's OpenAI account data controls. This application itself does not train or fine-tune AI models on QuickBooks data. These static pages are hosted by GitHub and are also subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Control and deletion

The owner may revoke the connection in QuickBooks and remove local token and report files. This website does not receive OAuth codes, tokens, or accounting records.

Contact

Use the public support issue tracker. Do not post credentials or financial data in public issues.
